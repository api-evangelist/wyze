The threaded holes on the bottom (normally for the solar panel) are probably your best bet. You can get thread adapters on amazon to reduce your 1/4" to whatever those are. Only other idea that comes to mind is to remove the grey mount and epoxy a 1/4" nut (may have to stack a couple) on the back of the cam.
