@Thomas-154 You hinted at something in one of your earlier posts that may point to a troubleshooting idea: If you’re router is dual-band 2.4/5 GHz (most are…mine is) you may want to try disabling the 5 GHz band (temporarily) while doing the V4 setup. I’ve had to do this once or twice with one of my V3 cams that wouldn’t find my wi-fi. Consistently, this was the only way I could get it to work.
