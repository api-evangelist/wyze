There was a time when getting a home security system felt like inviting a small army into your living room. You had to schedule a "consultation," wait for a technician to show up in a four-hour window they inevitably missed, and then watch them drill holes in your walls while charging you by the hour. It was expensive, intrusive, and about as modern as a PalmPilot .
